Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Basic Rule

  • Serve – starts the rally.
  • Pass (Bump) – receives the ball.
  • Set – places the ball for a teammate.
  • Spike – powerful attack hit.
  • Block – stops an opponent's attack at the net.

Equipment

  • Volleyball

  • Whistle
  • Knee pads
